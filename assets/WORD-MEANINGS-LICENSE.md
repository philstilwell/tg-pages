# Familiar-word English meanings

The dictionary meanings in `word-meanings-data.js` are an extract from the English portion of **JMdict**, copyright **James William Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG)**. Downloaded on October 17, 2026.

- Source: https://www.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMdict_e.gz
- Project and documentation: https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html
- EDRDG licence and attribution requirements: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
- Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

This extract is distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Changes: limited to words and possible base forms in the Tokyo Girl course; selected English glosses, reading restrictions, and common-entry priority; some senses omitted for a compact display. Entry IDs are retained. Dictionary meanings are general definitions, not guaranteed translations of every occurrence in the series. No endorsement by EDRDG is implied.

Lesson-specific meanings, grammatical explanations, and proper-name explanations are maintained separately in `word-meanings.js` and the existing course study guides. Learners' own saved meanings remain in their browser/account and are not bundled with the site.

To refresh the extract, download the current source above and run `python3 scripts/build-word-meanings.py /path/to/JMdict_e.gz`, update the download date in this notice, and run the project checks. Refresh the dictionary extract at least monthly when maintaining the live site.
